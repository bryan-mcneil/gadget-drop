Samsung has locked in July 22 for its next Galaxy Unpacked, and if you've been hovering over the buy button on a Galaxy Watch or a Galaxy Z foldable, that date is your cue to sit tight. New hardware lands in nine days, which means today's models are about to get either replaced or discounted.

## What Samsung Confirmed for the July 22 Galaxy Unpacked

Samsung's own newsroom posted the invitation this week. The event runs July 22 in London, streaming on Samsung.com and YouTube at 9 a.m. EDT (2 p.m. BST). The teaser carries the tagline "A New Shape Unfolds," and the animation resolves into the Galaxy AI logo. That's the full list of what Samsung has officially said.

Everything else is leaks, and it helps to separate the two. Reporting from SamMobile and others points to three new foldables, likely the Galaxy Z Flip 8 and a redesigned Galaxy Z Fold 8 that's rumored to be shorter and wider than before. On the wearable side, a new Galaxy Watch generation and a Watch Ultra follow-up are widely expected to share the stage, with an outside chance Samsung shows its Android XR smart glasses. None of those product names are confirmed by Samsung, so treat the specifics as informed guesses until the stream goes live.

## What a New Unpacked Means for Buyers

For most people reading this, the practical takeaway is simple: a countdown just started on the current lineup. When Samsung launches a new Galaxy Watch, the outgoing model doesn't vanish. It usually gets a price cut while stock lasts, and that's when the previous generation becomes one of the best-value smartwatches you can buy.

That's exactly the position the Galaxy Watch 7 is in. It already delivers flagship health tracking and Wear OS smarts for well under $200, and our [Samsung Galaxy Watch 7 review](/posts/samsung-galaxy-watch-7-review-flagship-smarts-under-200) walks through why it has held up. Samsung has committed to years of Wear OS and security updates on that generation, so a Watch 7 bought on discount won't feel abandoned the day its successor ships. Once the next model is official, expect the Watch 7 to slide further in price rather than disappear. If you don't need the newest sensor or chip, that discount is the real opportunity here, not the shiny new watch.

For a new watch to earn full price over a discounted Watch 7, it would need a real jump in battery life or a genuinely new health sensor, and those are the two things to watch for on the 22nd. A slightly faster chip or a fresh case color won't be worth the gap between launch pricing and a clearance-priced last-gen model.

The foldable news matters less to GadgetDrop's world, but the pattern is the same. A new Z Flip and Z Fold arriving means retailers clear the older ones, so anyone who wanted last year's foldable at a discount will get a better window in August than they have today.

There's a catch to waiting, though. Launch-day pricing on Samsung's newest watches rarely includes a discount, and preorder bonuses like bands, store credit, or storage bumps can make buying the new model at full price feel reasonable if you were going to upgrade anyway. Waiting pays off most when you specifically want the outgoing generation on sale.

## Buy or Wait?

Wait. With a confirmed Samsung event nine days out, buying a current Galaxy Watch at full price today is the one move that doesn't make sense. Either the new model tempts you, or the Watch 7 you were eyeing gets cheaper. Both outcomes favor holding off until July 22.

The only reason to buy right now is a genuine emergency replacement, a watch that broke and can't wait. For everyone else, keep the money in your pocket for nine days. Watch the reveal, check where the older models land, then decide. The worst case is that you buy the same Watch 7 in August for less than it costs this morning.
